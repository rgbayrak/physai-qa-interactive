# User guide

The tool has two tabs at the top of the page:

- **Explore and Annotate** steps through every scan of a dataset, shows its physiological signals with head-motion confounds and task events, and saves your annotations.
- **Stats** compares signal quality metrics across the whole dataset, one scan at a time. This tab only appears once the quality metrics are computed (see [Computing the metrics](quality-metrics.md#computing-the-metrics)).
